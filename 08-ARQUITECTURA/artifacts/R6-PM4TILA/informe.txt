==============================================================================
pm4tila 1.1.0 -- la longitud de un paquete PM4, medida
==============================================================================

ORACULO 1, GEOMETRICO: un anillo PM4 es una tilesion EXACTA. La suma
         de las longitudes tiene que dar el numero de dwords del anillo.
         No depende de ningun nombre de opcode ni de ninguna cabecera.

ESPACIO DE CONVENCIONES (declarado, es parte del contrato):
   PACKET3=count+1    nop=1dword PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=count+1    nop=1dword PACKET2=count+1   
   PACKET3=count+1    nop=regla PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=count+1    nop=regla PACKET2=count+1   
   PACKET3=count+2    nop=1dword PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=count+2    nop=1dword PACKET2=count+1   
   PACKET3=count+2    nop=regla PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=count+2    nop=regla PACKET2=count+1   
   PACKET3=2*count+1  nop=1dword PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=2*count+1  nop=1dword PACKET2=count+1   
   PACKET3=2*count+1  nop=regla PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=2*count+1  nop=regla PACKET2=count+1   
   PACKET3=2*count+2  nop=1dword PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=2*count+2  nop=1dword PACKET2=count+1   
   PACKET3=2*count+2  nop=regla PACKET2=2*count+1 
   PACKET3=2*count+2  nop=regla PACKET2=count+1   
   (16 combinaciones)
